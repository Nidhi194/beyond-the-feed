# 📱 Beyond the Feed

> **Looking beyond likes, reels, and trends.**

Beyond the Feed is a digital magazine that explores Instagram's influence on modern life through thoughtful stories on creativity, online communities, digital habits, and mindful social media use. Instead of viewing social media as entirely good or bad, the platform encourages a balanced perspective on how Instagram shapes the way people create, connect, learn, and express themselves.

Designed with a clean editorial layout and inspired by modern digital publications, the project focuses on delivering an engaging reading experience while presenting meaningful content around one of the world's most influential social platforms.

## 🎯 Project Vision

Social media has become part of everyday life, influencing the way people communicate, discover ideas, build businesses, and form communities. Beyond the Feed aims to explore these experiences through informative, visually engaging content that encourages curiosity rather than judgment.
